First public release.

- SMS firewall inside the phone service: Allow, Hold or Block for each message type, presets and custom rules.
- Blocks location requests, SIM data download, settings pushes and malformed WAP push (CVE-2026-58882) by default.
- Records every hidden message with an explanation and the decoded fields.
- Rules apply from boot, before the first unlock.
- No internet permission. Encrypted database.
